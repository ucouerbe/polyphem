# Plan

Write the plan to PLAN.md at the root of the worktree, in English, in this format:

## Goal
One or two sentences: what changes for the user.

## Approach
The chosen approach and why, plus the alternatives you rejected.

## Steps
A numbered list. Each step is small, verifiable, and names the files it touches.

## Risks and open questions
What could go wrong. Questions you need answered before starting.

## How to test
Commands and manual checks that prove it works.

Do not start implementing until the plan is approved.
